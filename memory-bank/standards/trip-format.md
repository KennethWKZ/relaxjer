# Trip format

A trip is a folder (`trips/<slug>/`, or `examples/demo-trip/` for the committed example) holding `data.js`, the
`*.json` side files and `img/`. `pnpm test` checks it against the contract in `tests/support/trip-contract.mjs`
(`TRIP_DIR=trips/<slug> pnpm test`). This page covers the settings the engine reads; `examples/demo-trip/data.js` shows
every field in use.

trip's own currency symbol and group size; the page adds the per-person share. `**bold**` works in all text.

### A day that splits (`DAYS[i].split`)

When one or some of the group do something else for part of a day (a ride, a visit), the plan hangs, folded, on the
day's string where it forks, and the stop where they come back says so
([ADR-20261001-day-split](decisions/ADR-20261001-day-split.md)). That stop reads `Rejoining here: <who> (<plans>)`, so
`who` can name one person or several. A push-back moves the fork and each plan's rejoin with the day's stops; the times inside each plan stay as planned
([ADR-20261007-split-follows-pushback](decisions/ADR-20261007-split-follows-pushback.md)).
Which plan a phone shows is remembered on that phone and isn't synced.

| Field     | What it is                                                                                |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `at`      | `'13:45'`: where it forks; it hangs after the last stop that starts by then               |
| `who`     | `[zh, en]`, for example `['一人', 'One of us']`; never a family relationship              |
| `h`       | `[zh, en]`: what they do instead                                                          |
| `sub`     | `[zh, en]`, optional: when it applies and what it skips                                   |
| `icon`    | optional sprite icon, `bike` by default                                                   |
| `go`      | the decision rows, `[{ k: 'go' \| 'wait' \| 'stop', name: [zh, en] }, …]`                 |
| `options` | 1–4 plans, the switch between them (one may be `default: true`); each plan's fields below |
| `lists`   | optional `[{ h: [zh, en], list: [[zh, en], …] }, …]`: the bike, what to carry             |
| `note`    | `[zh, en]`, optional: where the numbers come from (mark estimates "≈")                    |

A plan (`options[]`): `id`, `name: [zh, en]`, and `join: 'HH:MM'`, the start time of the stop where they come back
(after `at`). Everything else is optional and shows only when present:

- `km`, `ride` and `stops` (minutes), `leave` and `back` (`'HH:MM'`), and `fee: [zh, en]`;
- `start`, `end` and `alt` (a second place to return the bike): `{ name: [zh, en], lat, lng, yb }`, where `yb` is
  the bike-share station number, and the page shows its live bikes and docks when the plan opens;
- `via`: up to 3 `[lat, lng]` points for the directions link (Google Maps, by bike, from `start` to `end`);
- `line`: `[[lat, lng], …]`, the route drawn dashed in the day's colour on the map. Take it from a free router
  (OpenStreetMap bike routing), never from Google's;
- `rule: [zh, en]` (a turn-back time, a cut-off), `rows: [[[zh, en], [zh, en]], …]` (more key / value rows), and
  `place` (a `PLACES` key, for a plan that's a visit).

| `shops`  | `true`: the trip's shop box (`shops.json`) hangs under this stop                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `step`   | a step number shown instead of a clock time (with the day's `stepsTitle`), for a free day's ideas; a step is never pushed back                                                                                                                                                             |

### Day cards (`DAYS[i].blocks[]`)

Each card is `{ type, h: [zh, en], icon, … }`. `places` (place keys) and `sites` (`SITES` keys) add link rows to most.
On the page each card is a tile under the day's card, an icon over a short word, that opens it in a sheet
([ADR-20261128-progressive-disclosure](decisions/ADR-20261128-progressive-disclosure.md)). The word is the card's
`tile: [zh, en]` (at most 6 characters in Chinese and 16 in English), else its heading when that's short, else the
word for its type (Spend, Rain plan, Wear…). The sheet opens with one line worked out from the card (how many
options; a table's rows with the range of its main price column, named, or its columns; the total); `sum: [zh, en]`
replaces it when the planner wants their own words. On a `decide` card, `pick: '<k>'` marks the option the group has chosen: the summary
names it with a "Chosen" mark, and `pick` must be one of the card's `opts[].k`.

A `decide` option that goes somewhere can carry its own `route`, the same legs as `DAYS[i].route`
(`[[from place, to place, mode, 'HH:MM'?, minutes?], …]`); the option's card shows it as a route line with each leg's
minutes, a walk at the group's pace ([ADR-20261127-decide-option-route](decisions/ADR-20261127-decide-option-route.md)).
The data refresh asks Google for its legs and stores them in `geo.json` `legs` as `<day id>:<k>:<i>`, apart from the
day's own `<day id>:<i>`, so its `k` is letters, digits, `_` or `-`, and no other routed option of that day has it.
A leg without its own time sets off at the nearest earlier time in the option's route, else at the option's first; an
option with no time at all takes the day route's defaults (09:30 first leg, 20:30 last, 13:30 between). The schedule's
drive times don't apply: an option has no schedule. The whole-minutes element works as on a day leg.

| `type`        | Shows                                                                                           | Fields                                                                        |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `text`        | paragraphs, an optional total with its per-person share                                         | `p: [[zh, en], …]`, `total: { amt, per, min, max }`, `link: [href, [zh, en]]` |
| `list`        | a bullet list                                                                                   | `list`, `note`                                                                |
| `kv`, `costs` | label / value rows (`costs` for prices)                                                         | `rows: [[[zh, en], [zh, en]], …]`, `note`                                     |
| `table`       | a table                                                                                         | `cols`, `rows`                                                                |
| `route`       | a line of stations or stops, with metro line colours                                            | `stops: [{ name, line, code, isLine }]`, `note`, `total`, `note2`, `plain`    |
| `decide`      | options A / B to pick on the day, each with its cost and rule, and its own place and site links | `opts: [{ k, mark, name, cost, when, list, place, site, route }]`, `pick`     |
| `weather`     | go / wait / stop rows for a weather-dependent plan                                              | `opts: [{ k: 'go' \| 'wait' \| 'stop', name, list, body }]`, `flow`           |
| `wear`        | what to wear and bring                                                                          | `main`, `bring`, `note`                                                       |
| `budget`      | the day's costs, each with its share, and a total                                               | `rows: [[[zh, en], amount, …]]`, `total`, `min`, `max`, `est`, `note`         |
| `rain`        | the rain plan                                                                                   | `list`, or `groups: [{ h, list }]`                                            |
| `checklist`   | ticks kept on the phone                                                                         | `id`, `items: [[zh, en], …]`                                                  |
| `groups`      | two side-by-side lists                                                                          | `groups: [{ h, list }]`, `note`                                               |

Days can be any number, ids `d1`…`dN` in order. The engine reads each day's role from the data (`Plan.dayRoles`):
the first day is the arrival; the day the group leaves for the airport is the evening before an after-midnight
take-off, else the flight's own day; a day after it that holds only the early take-off takes no added stops.

## Places, sites and the airport section

The engine looks up no place or site by a fixed name, with one exception still in the engine: keep a `hotel` key,
because Today's hotel fact and the route card's "back to the hotel" read `PLACES.hotel`. The hotel is
`PLACES[TRIP.hotel]` (default `'hotel'`), or one per night (`DAYS[i].hotel`); every
other place or site is named by the data that uses it, and the contract checks that each one exists.

| Field                                                         | Example                                              | What it drives                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `TRIP.hotel`                                                  | `'hotel'`                                            | the hotel's place id when one hotel serves the whole trip                                                                                                                  |
| `DAYS[i].hotel`                                               | `'onsen'`                                            | a trip with several hotels: the hotel from that night on (a day without one keeps the night before's). The airport evening uses the last night's hotel, where the bags are |
| `AIRPORT.terminals`                                           | `[{ place: 'tpe1', code: 'T1' }, …]`                 | the arrival and going-home route links; the first is where the group lands and leaves                                                                                      |
| `AIRPORT.sites`, `AIRPORT.departSites`                        | `['tymetro']`, `[{ site: 'uber', label: [zh, en] }]` | site links under the arrival steps and the going-home links                                                                                                                |
| `AIRPORT.route`, `routeTitle`, `routeAlt`                     |                                                      | the rail diagram from the airport, its heading and its accessible title                                                                                                    |
| `AIRPORT.transitCard`, `transitCardSite`, `transitCardTicket` | list, `'easycard'`, `'easycard-buy'`                 | the transit-card block (its name comes from the region pack, `Pack.transitCard`)                                                                                           |
| `AIRPORT.methods[].ticket`                                    | `'airport-transfer-booking'`                         | an `extra.json` ticket card shown with that way to the hotel                                                                                                               |
| `MONEY.transitCard`                                           | `[zh, en]`                                           | the transit card in the budget's cash-and-cards block                                                                                                                      |
| `WEATHER.sites`, `ENTRY.sites`, `ENTRY.lucky.sites`           | `['cwa', 'cwaEn']`                                   | site links under the forecast, the entry rules and the lucky draw                                                                                                          |
| `ENTRY.sources`                                               | `[zh, en]`                                           | "Sources: …" under the entry rules                                                                                                                                         |

### A place (`PLACES[key]`)

| Field            | What it drives                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `name`           | `[zh, en]`                                                                                                                                  |
| `maps`           | the text a map or directions link searches for                                                                                              |
| `trad`           | the destination's own spelling (Traditional Chinese in Taiwan, Japanese in Japan): search, the "show the driver" card, and the native layer |
| `roma`           | its romanization (Hepburn in Japan): shown beside `trad` in the native layer and on the driver card (pack `native`)                         |
| `addr`, `addrEn` | the address in the local language and in English (the driver card, the hotel fact)                                                          |
| `tel`, `note`    | the phone number (the driver card), and a note (`[zh, en]`)                                                                                 |
| `site`           | the place's own website                                                                                                                     |
| `gpid`           | a hand-checked Google place id, pinned so the data refresh can't swap it for a neighbour                                                    |
| `gname`          | the name Google Maps still lists, when the place renamed: the hotel fact warns that a search for the new one misleads                       |

Positions and hours aren't written here: the data refresh puts them in `geo.json` (`data-sync`).

### The budget's rows (`BUDGET.rows`)

The Budget view leads with what the group has paid, in the home currency, and one line for what's still to come and
each person's share of it ([ADR-20261128-budget-paid-insurance](decisions/ADR-20261128-budget-paid-insurance.md)). A
row is one of four shapes:

| Row                   | Shape                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| An estimate           | `[label, text, min, max, anchor?, cat?]`: a range in the trip's currency, as before; `anchor: 'airport'` links the airport note; its kind last (`[…, 'airport', 'transport']`, or `[…, 'food']`) |
| A payment             | `{ label, min, max, cur, paid, via, ref, est, anchor }`: `paid` is the day it was paid (YYYY-MM-DD), and then `min` = `max`, one exact amount; without `paid` it's still to come                 |
| A payment in parts    | `{ label, parts: [{ amt, cur, paid, via, label }] }`: a deposit now and the balance later (`paid` on the parts already paid); the row has no `min`, `max` or `paid` of its own                   |
| A price not known yet | `{ label, tbd: true, via }`: listed under Still to pay as "price TBD", never counted in a total                                                                                                  |

- `cur`: `'home'` (or the home code, `TRIP.currency.home`) for something paid at home, such as flights; left out, the
  trip's currency. Sums are shown in the home currency, each converted figure marked ≈.
- `cat`: the row's kind for the budget's By kind sheet, one of `transport` (flights, trains, transfers, a car with its
  driver), `car` (a car the group drives: rental, fuel, parking, tolls), `lodging`, `food`, `tickets`, `activity` (ski
  lifts, lessons, gear rental), `insurance`, `other`. On an object row it's `cat`; on an estimate it's the last item, after
  the anchor when there is one. A row without one counts as `other`, except an estimate anchored at the airport, which
  counts as `transport`; the page never guesses a kind from the words.
- `via`: where it was or will be paid, `[zh, en]` or a name ("Booking site", "Cash on the day"). `ref`: the booking
  number, shown only when the row is opened.
- `excludes` keeps what's truly outside the plan (shopping); flights and lodging can be rows. A budget of estimate rows
  only keeps `total`, `totalMin` and `totalMax`, and the contract checks they add up.

### The tax refund (`TAX`)

A tile in Today's bookings opens it ([ADR-20261128-tax-refund](decisions/ADR-20261128-tax-refund.md)); the minimum spend
comes from the country pack (`taxRefund.min`). Every field is optional but `h`; an old `TAX` (`h`, `list`, `shops`,
`note`) keeps working.

| Field           | Holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `h`             | its heading, `[zh, en]`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `list`          | the rules in short, `[[zh, en], …]`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `steps`         | how it works, in order, `[[zh, en], …]` (show the passport and pay the taxed price, keep the receipt's QR code, card or cash, customs at the airport before check-in, the refund)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `methods`       | `[{ h, items, note? }]`: to a card, cash at the airport, each with its rules (which cards, registration, how long)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `airport`       | `{ h?, rows: [{ what, where, when?, note? }], wifi?: { ssid, pass?, note? } }`: the customs kiosk, the cash machine and where they are; the customs Wi-Fi with a Copy button for its name and password                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `deadline`      | `[zh, en]`: how long after buying the group can claim                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `coupons`       | `[{ id, shop, h, items?, until?, img?, rules?, place? }]`: a shop's coupon: `id` a word, `shop` `[zh, en]` or a name, `items` its tiers, `until` its last day (YYYY-MM-DD; after it, it greys and folds to Expired; left out when the coupon prints none: it never expires and says 没写有效期 / No end date shown, never a made-up date), `img` a trip image key, `rules`, `place` a `PLACES` key or a shop list's id |
| `src`           | `[zh, en]`: where it comes from, with dates                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `shops`, `note` | which shops offer it; a closing note                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

A coupon's `img` is a key under the trip's `img/` folder, such as `'tax/coupon-store'` for `img/tax/coupon-store.webp`
(or `.png`, `.jpg`, `.svg`). The build puts it in the page at its full size, never a lighter copy, so the cashier's
scanner reads its barcode; tapped, it opens full screen on white (a landscape one turned to fill an upright phone), and a double tap zooms in. The contract checks
the image is there. A coupon image is a few hundred KB; the build's summary counts coupons apart from the photos.

### Travel insurance (`INSURANCE`)

Optional. A tile in Today's bookings opens its sheet.

| Field      | What it holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `policies` | a list: `name` and `plan` `[zh, en]`, `insurer` (the company), `who` `[zh, en]` ("everyone, same plan"), `from` and `to` (YYYY-MM-DD), `covers` (a list of `[zh, en]`), `note`, `site` (a `SITES` key or an https link), `app: { name, note }`, `contacts` |
| `contacts` | per policy: `label` and `when` `[zh, en]` ("24-hour line", "a hospital abroad"), `tel` (a tel: link), `wa` (WhatsApp, a wa.me link); the insurer's numbers, and the group's own agent when the planner asks; never an ID or home address                   |
| `claims`   | what to keep for a claim, by event: `[{ h, items }]`, each `[zh, en]` ("Flight delay": the original boarding passes, the airline's letter with date, time and cause)                                                                                       |

**No personal identifiers.** The page is shared with the whole group: no ID or passport numbers, addresses or birth
dates, in `INSURANCE` or anywhere. The contract allows only the fields above and refuses keys such as `ic`, `nric`,
`passport`, `dob` or `address`. A rental's cover is just another policy.

### Water refill (`refill.json`)

Written by the `refill` step ([ADR-20261128-water-refill](decisions/ADR-20261128-water-refill.md)); the build reads
it as `REFILL` and leaves every refill part out without it. `{ checked: 'YYYY-MM-DD', spots: [ … ] }`, each spot:

| Field                  | Holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `id`, `src`            | a unique id, prefixed with its source (`osm-n123`, `<src>-…`), and the source's short name                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `name`, `address`      | as the source gives them, or `null` (the page then says "Water tap", "Refill shop" or "Indoor water")                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `lat`, `lng`           | its position                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `kind`                 | `tap` (a fountain or a tap), `shop` (a shop or café that fills a bottle) or `shelter` (a public indoor place)                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `indoor`, `seasonal`   | `true`, `false` or `null`. `indoor: true` keeps a tap on a winter day, `false` (outdoors) shuts it there, and `null` (not known) counts as outdoors in a pack's winter; a shop or a shelter is indoors. `seasonal: true` is shut for winter (OSM `seasonal=*`, in December to February without a pack rule), `false` open all year |
| `hours`, `hours_note`  | `{ days: { "1": [["10:00", "22:00"]], … "7": [] }, known }` ("1" is Monday, an empty list closed, a range may run past midnight) or `null`; the words when the hours can't be read                                                                                                                                                 |
| `water`, `how`, `site` | what water it gives (`tap`, `cold`, `hot`, `filtered`, or the source's own words), how to ask, and a link to the spot's page                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `added`, `credit`      | when the source added it, and whose data it is, shown with it (`© OpenStreetMap contributors` for OpenStreetMap's)                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

A trip's `refill-<src>.json` holds `spots` in the same shape; fields of the source's own (a category, its nearest place)
are dropped. The page works out what's near a stop (600 m), what's open then, and what winter shuts: on a day inside the
pack's `refillWinter` every tap that isn't `indoor: true` or `seasonal: false` is left out and counted.

### Rest stops along a long drive (`drive-stops.json`)

Written by the `drive-stops` step ([ADR-20261128-drive-stops](decisions/ADR-20261128-drive-stops.md)) for each day's
drive of 90 minutes or more; the build reads it as `DRIVE_STOPS`, and without it the page shows no stops. They are for
reference: the page never plans one or moves a time. `{ checked: 'YYYY-MM-DD', src: 'Google Maps', legs: { … } }`, each
leg under its day's key (`d5:0`) with the `from`, `to` and `mode` (`driving`) it was found for, so a day reordered since
shows none; its `min` (Google's time, typical traffic) and `km`; and `stops`, in order along the way:

| Field                                 | Holds                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `name`, `kind`                        | the name as Google gives it in the trip's language; `sa` (a service area), `pa` (a parking area), `roadside` (a roadside station), `fuel` or `store`  |
| `km`, `min`                           | how far in from the start, and the leg's minutes shared out by distance (Google's pace)                                                               |
| `lat`, `lng`                          | its position, for the Directions button                                                                                                               |
| `h24`, `shut`, `hours`                | on the drive's day: `h24: true` open 24 hours, `shut: true` closed, or its times (`'07:00–22:00'`, more than one joined with `, `); none when unknown |
| `dir`                                 | `up` or `down` when the name carries the expressway's direction word (上り, 下り): the twin on the side the car keeps to                              |
| `rating`, `reviews`, `rating_checked` | Google's rating, its count and the date read (the page doesn't show them; a demo carries none)                                                        |

The page folds them under the row the group is in while it drives: the "Drive to …" row at the destination, or the row
it leaves from. On the day, from the time the drive sets off (the end of the row before its arrival) until it should
have arrived, the stop ahead by the clock is marked Next.
