# Surface brief: site/index.html

Scope: RelaxJer's GitHub Pages landing page (static HTML + CSS, no build), linking to the repo. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: planners (often not developers) deciding whether to plan a family trip with this repo; contributors second. Job: understand in one viewport that RelaxJer is one offline phone page for the whole group, planned with an AI agent; see the real page work; take the four steps. Constraints: never a real trip (demo captures only), Google Maps recommended with a link to the guide, both themes, 44 px targets, the affordance rules in memory-bank/standards/patterns/affordances.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The plan is the pitch. Planning is shown as a trip: four lanterns hung on one string, each a step, with the working instructions printed plainly underneath, in the trip page's own grammar. It refuses the SaaS template of headline, grid of feature cards and CTA band.

OWN-WORLD: DESIGN.md's lantern paper. White paper by day, a night sky with glowing lanterns by night; sumi ink; brush lettering only for the brand and each step's one-word wish; system sans for everything operated; seal red only on the one fixed promise; lantern colours 1–4 own the four steps; the string threads them; real 390 px captures of the synthetic demo are the proof.

STORY: A planner arrives from the README or a chat link, sees the real page (a day lantern, now and next, per-person costs), follows four steps with Google recommended, and opens the repo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Translucent sticky bar (brush RelaxJer; How it works, Google Maps, GitHub). Left: brush RelaxJer at display scale, "relax jer: Malaysian for just relax", one-sentence lede, ink go button "Plan your trip" and a link to the guide. Right, and below on phones: the day-3 lantern capture in a phone frame, the Now/Next capture tucked behind it.

FORM: code-led; structure 1 of 5 (steps as lanterns on a string); seed key: none (concept-seed and the interview skipped under the user's standing act-don't-ask instruction).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
