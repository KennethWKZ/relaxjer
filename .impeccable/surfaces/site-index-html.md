# Surface brief: site/index.html

Scope: RelaxJer's GitHub Pages landing page (static HTML + CSS + one small script, no build), linking to the repo. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: planners (often not developers) deciding whether to plan a group trip with this repo; contributors second. Job: understand in one viewport that RelaxJer is one offline phone page for the whole group, planned with an AI agent; watch each feature work on the demo trip; take the four steps. Constraints: never a real trip (the synthetic demo trip is the only content source), Google Maps recommended with a link to the guide, both themes, 44 px targets, the affordance rules in memory-bank/standards/patterns/affordances.md, motion pausable and still under reduced motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every feature arrives as a postcard from the trip. The picture side is the trip page at work (a looping demo screen of the synthetic demo trip); the back is written plainly. It refuses the SaaS hero, the grid of identical feature cards and the CTA band.

OWN-WORLD: Airmail under one slow trip day. The ground is the day's sky: a morning sun at the top, clouds and birds, an afternoon gold through lilac, and lantern night in the footer (stars, a hill ridge, lanterns rising); the night theme is the same page under stars with lanterns in the margins. Lanterns keep to the side lanes, never behind a word; white postcard stock (dark stock at night) with a soft resting shadow; red and blue airmail chevrons on edges; perforated stamps carrying lanterns in the trip page's lantern colours; round postmarks in ink; the brush face for the brand and the brushed Chinese line on each card; a self-hosted friendly geometric sans for headlines; system sans for everything operated.

STORY: A planner arrives, reads "one page for the whole trip" on a postcard with the real page lying on it, watches the postcards replay the demo trip (what's next, fixed times, costs per person, food and toilets, optional plans, a split day, group sync, offline, privacy), follows four steps with Google recommended, and opens the repo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Translucent sticky bar (brush RelaxJer; Features, How it works, a sun/moon theme button, GitHub). Left: brush RelaxJer at display scale, "relax jer: Malaysian for just relax", a one-sentence lede, an ink go button "Plan your trip" and a stock one of the same size, "Try the demo trip" (the Google guide waits in the second step). Right (below on phones): the back of a postcard, tilted, with a brushed line, an address block "To: everyone in the group chat", a lantern stamp and a postmark, and a phone lying across it showing the real Now/Next capture.

FORM: code-led; candidate 6 of 7 (postcards from the trip; the list ran sky lanterns, station stamp rally, metro map, travel tickets, kopitiam table, postcards, night-market signage); seed key d20f5e1e. Signature interaction: the airmail route, where an added stop flies as a letter along a dashed arc from one phone to the other. Motion grammar: the sky moves only with the scroll (the sun keeps to the screen's corner and sets, warming, as the page nears night, and a moon rises over the footer; clouds and birds drift, lanterns rise), never by itself; postcards settle once on arrival, postmarks press, demo screens loop only while visible, every loop has a Pause button (an icon-only paper disc under the phone, never over the screen) and holds a still frame under reduced motion. Raises: one full-strength spot colour per postcard (from the layout-desk challenger); a shared change visibly lands on the second phone (from the minihompy challenger).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
